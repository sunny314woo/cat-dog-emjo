Which pets can become stickers?

One cat or one dog per pack. Choose a photo with just your pet, with their face and markings easy to see.

Can I see my pack before I pay?

Yes. First, preview your pet in four styles. Pick your favorite to see all nine stickers with a visible watermark. Pay only when you’re happy with the preview.

Are the final stickers transparent?

Yes. The paid pack includes clean transparent PNGs without watermarks, cut into nine separate files, plus the complete sheet.

How do I use them in my chats?

Your pack includes WeChat-size PNGs and WhatsApp-size files. Save and import them using your preferred sticker tool. Automatic installation into messaging apps is not included.

Do I need an account?

No account or subscription. Your email is used for your order and to deliver your finished pack.
